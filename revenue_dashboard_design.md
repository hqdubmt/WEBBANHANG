## 3. KPI Strip (Top Bar)

| KPI | Formula | Data Source | Status |
|-----|---------|-------------|--------|
| Revenue Today | SUM(orders.total WHERE date=today) | /api/orders/revenue | CO |
| Revenue MTD | SUM(orders.total WHERE month=current) | /api/analytics/revenue | CO |
| Orders Today | COUNT(orders WHERE date=today) | /api/orders | CO |
| Avg Order Value | totalRevenue / orderCount | Computed | CO |
| New Customers MTD | COUNT(customers WHERE month=current) | /api/analytics/customers | CO |
| Active Leads | COUNT(leads WHERE status IN [new,contacted,qualified]) | /api/leads | CO |
