
# EXECUTIVE_AI_CEO_V2.md

## MISSION

Xây dựng Executive AI CEO — tầng điều hành chiến lược cao nhất của AI Social Commerce OS.

Executive AI CEO KHÔNG thay thế con người.

Executive AI CEO đóng vai trò:

- CEO AI: nhìn thấy toàn bộ doanh nghiệp, ra quyết định chiến lược
- COO AI: theo dõi operations, phát hiện bottleneck
- Chief Revenue Officer AI: tối ưu revenue engine
- Chief Growth Officer AI: tìm cơ hội tăng trưởng

# BOTTLENECK ANALYSIS

`ExecutiveDecisionService.analyzeBottlenecks()`:

```typescript
interface ExecutiveBottleneck {
  location: 'traffic' | 'lead' | 'sales' | 'revenue' | 'retention' | 'support';
  severity: 'critical' | 'high' | 'medium';
  metric: string;
  currentValue: number;
  targetValue: number;
  impactOnRevenue: number;   // estimated revenue loss per day
  rootCause: string;
  assignedModule: string;
  recommendedAction: string;
}
```

Logic:
- Traffic bottleneck: leads/day < 5
- Lead bottleneck: sales qualified < 20% of leads
- Sales bottleneck: order/lead ratio < 5%
- Revenue bottleneck: revenue < 80% of target
- Retention bottleneck: churn > 10%/month
- Support bottleneck: unresolved tickets > 48h

# RISK MANAGEMENT

`RiskManagementService.detectRisks()`:

Cron: `@Cron('0 */4 * * *')` — mỗi 4 giờ.

```typescript
interface ExecutiveRisk {
  type: 'revenue_decline' | 'profit_decline' | 'customer_churn' | 
        'campaign_failure' | 'operational_risk' | 'growth_stall';
  severity: 'critical' | 'high' | 'medium' | 'low';
  description: string;
  metric: string;
  threshold: number;
  currentValue: number;
  estimatedImpact: string;
  mitigationAction: string;
  assignedModule: string;
}
```

Risk detection thresholds:
- Revenue decline: > 20% DoD
- Profit decline: margin < 10%
- Customer churn: > 10%/month
- Campaign failure: all active campaigns ROI < 1x
- Operational risk: agent error rate > 5%
- Growth stall: MoM growth < 0%
